# EPL 2024/25 Title Race — Cumulative Points by Matchweek

## Question
How did the top 6 EPL teams accumulate points across the 
2024/25 season — and when did Liverpool pull away?

## Data
Premier League match results 2021-2025.
Filtered to 2024/25 season · 38 matchweeks · Top 6 teams.

## Methodology
- Filtered dataset to 2024/25 season
- Calculated points per match (W=3, D=1, L=0)
- Calculated cumulative points matchweek by matchweek
- Plotted each team's title race journey in their 
  official club colours

## Key Findings
- Liverpool were the dominant force — pulling away 
  from the pack consistently from mid-season
- Arsenal were Liverpool's closest challengers 
  throughout but couldn't sustain the pressure
- Manchester City had a dramatically inconsistent 
  season compared to their previous title-winning form
- Newcastle and Tottenham battled for the final 
  Champions League places throughout
